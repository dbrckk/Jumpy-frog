# Jumpy Frog — Google Play listing content

## Title

Jumpy Frog: River Hop

## Short description (English)

Hop between lily pads, rescue cute friends and outrun the river wave!

## Long description (English)

Find your rhythm on a beautiful, winding river.

In Jumpy Frog, every leap counts. Pull back, aim, and release to hop between lily pads. Collect leaves, make new animal friends, and stay one jump ahead of the wave chasing you!

**Features**
- Quick, satisfying one-finger jumping controls
- An endless, procedurally generated river course
- Four cute companion creatures to meet
- Unlockable color styles using leaves earned by playing
- Animated lily pads, ripples, splashes and colorful scenery
- Local best scores and offline play
- No ads, in-app purchases, accounts or Internet connection

How far can you hop before the river catches you?
